Stacking & orientation

Smart mixed can choose a different posture and direction at each floor position, even for the same cable item. Each individual stack still contains identical drums in one posture. Spacer footprint must fit within the drum footprint. Full stack height, including base and spacers, must fit through the door.

Geometry only: use stacking and flange-down transport only where your manufacturer explicitly allows it. Prysmian’s general guide requires upright transport; flat transport is not universally suitable. Upright stacks need suitable cradles or supports; a spacer alone does not establish stability. Include all spacer/support weight in packing weight.

Know your drum

F = flange diameter (height when on edge).
B = barrel diameter.
W = clear width available for cable.
Outside width = W + two flange thicknesses + total protrusion allowance.

On edge: footprint F × outside width; height F. Flat on flange: footprint F × F; height outside width. On-edge drums can rotate 90° on the floor. No flange interleaving or mixed-item stacks.

Drum rules & cable-capacity formula

Starting assumptions only: replace these with your cable and drum manufacturer's values. All dimensions are mm; capacity must return metres.

The traverse constraint is W ≤ ratio × F, where W is clear winding width and F is flange diameter. The default ratio is 1.5 and applies to every proposed drum. Variables: od = cable OD; m = barrel multiplier; F, B, W as shown; c = radial freeboard; k = packing factor. Supports + − * / ^ and parentheses, with pi available. The default is an annular-volume estimate, not a certified winding calculation. If your rule gives a bending radius, convert it to a barrel diameter. The conservative check uses whole turns per layer and whole radial layers in square winding, and takes the lower of that estimate and your formula. It may understate close-packed winding; select formula only for your validated model. Barrel overrides are minimum diameters, not bend radii.
